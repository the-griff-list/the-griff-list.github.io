THE GRIFFLIST — SOUND AND ROCK METER UPDATE

Install: extract this ZIP and upload the included website files to the root of your existing GitHub Pages repository, replacing matching files. Commit to main and wait for Pages to publish.

- Attempts to start Title screen.mp3 immediately on page load. Browsers may block autoplay until the first tap; this is a browser restriction, not a website setting.

- Sets separate playback volumes for title music, background menu music, and each remaining effect so effects remain audible over the quieter menu loop.
- Menu music starts after entering the site; if title playback was blocked until the first tap, menu music begins after the title cue ends.

Sound files included: Title screen.mp3, Menu screen.mp3, cash.wav, highway_riseR.wav, notes_ripple_up_01.wav, scroll.wav, you_rockR.wav.
