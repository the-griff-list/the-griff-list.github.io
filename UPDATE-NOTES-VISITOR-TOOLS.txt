LIMITATIONS
- The stream health display is a basic browser-side indicator, not a full bitrate or server monitoring dashboard.
- Automatic show reset starts a new show session timestamp and makes the “This Show” favourites range start fresh. It does not reset the all-time or monthly counts.
- Sound effects are reused from the homepage, but a browser or in-app browser may suppress audio until a user interaction.
- No live Supabase project or actual camera broadcast was available for end-to-end verification. Test in a staging copy first.
