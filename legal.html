Privacy, Terms & Legal

Plain-language information about using the website, sending song requests, and supporting live music.

Privacy notice

The website may process the name you enter, requested song and artist, optional message, request status, and a private request-tracking token so the request can be displayed and checked. If you choose to remember your name, that preference is stored in this browser and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Payments are handled by the payment provider you choose. The website does not ask you to enter card numbers or banking passwords into the request form. Payment methods shown at checkout depend on provider settings, currency, location, browser, and device. Apple Pay and Google Pay availability through Ko-fi depends on a connected Stripe account and supporter compatibility. Payment providers may separately process account, device, transaction, and contact information under their own privacy policies.

Donation activity received from a payment provider may include the amount, currency, supporter name, message, payment type, and transaction time. Some of this information is visible only in the private administrator dashboard. The administrator may separately choose to publish a confirmed contribution on the public Supporter Wall using a nickname or alias, amount, and optional requested song. Use Anonymous Rocker unless the supporter has explicitly agreed to public display. Transaction references and payment dates are not displayed there.

Do not include sensitive personal, financial, medical, or account information in a song request or public message. Information is used to operate the request, live-show, support, and safety features of the site. The private administrator area uses Supabase authentication and stores a sign-in session in the browser; sign out when using a shared device. Website hosting, security, and payment services may also process technical information such as IP address, device/browser details, and transaction metadata under their own terms. To ask about access or deletion of information associated with a request, use the booking contact.

Donations & payments

Voluntary donations support the independent music project and its ongoing costs. A donation does not buy a song, guarantee that a song will be performed, or influence the running order unless a separate priority-request option is clearly selected.

The optional priority-request add-on is a separate paid feature, currently displayed as $5 CAD when available. It provides priority consideration, not a guarantee that a requested song can be performed. Payment methods and availability are shown at checkout.

The Grifflist is not presented as a registered charity. This website does not issue official charitable donation receipts, and payments made through it should not be treated as tax-deductible charitable gifts. Payment confirmation and any provider-issued transaction receipt are subject to the provider's terms.

Payment processing, refunds, disputes, and account records may also be subject to the terms of Ko-fi, PayPal, or another provider used at checkout. Contact the booking email if a payment appears to have been duplicated or incorrectly associated with a request.

Music, copyright & live streams

Song titles, artist names, and repertoire information are provided for identification and request purposes. Copyright and other rights in musical works and recordings remain with their respective owners. Listing a song does not mean The Grifflist owns or licenses that work.

Live performances, recorded music, livestreams, archived video, and online transmission can involve different rights and licensing requirements. Venues, event organizers, stream operators, and performers should confirm that the applicable public-performance, reproduction, synchronization, and online-use permissions are in place for their specific activity.

For Canadian music licensing information, consult SOCAN and Entandem. This page is general information, not a legal opinion or a substitute for advice about a particular performance or stream.

Venue names and event details may be used as factual references to help visitors identify a publicly advertised performance. The Grifflist is an independent artist website and is not operated by, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially affiliated with Hunter’s Ale House or its owners unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Venue names remain the property of their respective owners.

Event dates, times, menus, reservations, admission, age requirements, accessibility, promotions, and venue policies may change. The venue’s official website and event listings control those details. Visitors should verify information with the venue before making plans. No discount, special offer, referral arrangement, sponsorship, revenue share, or permission to use venue branding is implied by a listing on this website.

Any future co-promotion, contest, tracked referral, QR placement, or commercial partnership will be published only after the relevant parties approve the arrangement and its terms. Alcohol purchases are never required to use this website, submit a song request, participate in a poll, or make a voluntary donation. Please follow venue policies and make safe transportation choices.

Website use & availability

Requests, polls, livestreams, and other interactive features are provided as available. Network failures, venue conditions, equipment issues, copyright restrictions, safety considerations, or technical problems may affect availability. Do not rely on the website as an emergency service.

Do not attempt to access the private administrator area, interfere with the service, submit unlawful content, impersonate another person, or use the site to distribute malware or spam.
