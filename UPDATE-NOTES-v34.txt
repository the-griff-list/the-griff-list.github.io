THE GRIFFLIST — UPDATE v34

SEASONAL WHAT'S NEW
- Removed overlapping holiday announcement windows with an explicit winter-holiday schedule.
- Christmas announcement runs December 18–25 (inclusive).
- Boxing Day receives its own single-day announcement on December 26.
- New Year's Eve / New Year's Day share one announcement from December 27 through January 1 (inclusive).
- This prevents Christmas, Boxing Day and New Year announcements from competing during overlapping seven-day windows.
- Easter uses one Easter campaign beginning seven days before Easter Sunday; Good Friday is folded into that campaign to avoid competing announcements.
- Other Canadian holiday announcements use a seven-day lead-in through the holiday itself.
- Seasonal headlines now use short rhyme/near-rhyme pairings: Griffy-Doo, jingle crew, midnight crew, bunny crew, maple crew, etc.

SEASONAL ANIMATIONS
- Halloween and Thanksgiving: brief falling autumn leaves.
- Christmas: brief falling snowflakes.
- Easter: brief falling pastel eggs.
- Canada Day: brief falling maple leaves.
- Valentine's Day and Family Day: brief falling hearts.
- Other celebratory holidays: brief falling stars/sparkles.
- Respectful observances (Truth and Reconciliation Day and Remembrance Day) remain calm and have no celebratory particle animation.
- Effects run for approximately 2.6 seconds, only inside What's New, and are disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

- Preserved website root file structure; QA scripts remain in repository root, not in a qa folder.
