PS2-inspired sound file purposes — send one file for each role you want to use:
1. STARTUP / BOOT: one short sound that plays once when a visitor presses “PRESS ANY BUTTON TO ROCK”. Best: a short boot sting, around 2–8 seconds.
2. MENU AMBIENCE: optional loop that plays after entering the site. Best: a calm atmospheric menu loop, ideally 20–90 seconds and seamless.
3. MENU SELECT: short click/beep when a visitor selects a menu item or button.
4. CONFIRM / ACCEPT: short positive sound after a successful action, such as a song request being sent.
5. BACK / CANCEL: short lower or descending sound when closing a panel or backing out.
6. SECRET UNLOCK: a distinctive discovery / glitch sound for opening the hidden Secret Stage.
7. TRANSITION: a brief sweep or startup-like flourish for changing to a new section or opening the Time Machine.
8. ARCADE PICKUP: optional short arcade sound when collecting a guitar pick in the mini-game.

You do not need to send all eight. If you only have a few, prioritize 1 (startup), 2 (menu loop), 3 (select), 4 (confirm), and 6 (secret unlock). Please upload the audio files and tell me which number/purpose each file is for. WAV or MP3 is fine; short files are best for UI effects. Only send files you have permission to use.
