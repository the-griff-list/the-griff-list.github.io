THE GRIFFLIST - COMPACT PUBLIC UI PLAN

Current public layout
- Home focuses on song requests, catalog, tips, Rock-O-Meter, Watch Live, and a compact Explore link.
- Explore contains Crowd Favourites, Encore Vote, and the Rock Wall.
- The duplicate decade picker and seasonal random-song card are disabled while the final design is reviewed.
- Public pages share Day, Night, and 80s appearance modes. Admin remains separate.

Secret Menu audio
- One footer button opens the PS2-inspired Secret Menu.
- The PS2 boot-up sound plays once on entry.
- PS2 menu-select, pickup, transition, Lost Cassette, and randomized high-score effects play only for their matching actions.
- Homepage menu music pauses while Secret Menu is open and resumes on close if it was playing before.
- There is no Secret Menu background music track or sound-mode dropdown; PS2 sounds are the default.
- Short effects are kept lightweight. The existing homepage title/menu sounds remain separate.

Rock Wall and Supabase
- The Rock Wall displays only real confirmed tip records from public.confirmed_tips.
- No fake names or donation amounts are generated.
- Crowd Favourites requires the get_crowd_favourites() function and crowd_favourite_pins table in grifflist-next-update.sql.
- Run SQL migrations manually in Supabase if they have not already been applied. The ZIP cannot apply SQL to the live database itself.
